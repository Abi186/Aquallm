# Harvest

General guidance on harvest planning and harvest performance.

## When to harvest

Harvest time depends on the target size, market price, disease risk and pond condition. Vannamei are commonly harvested at around 90 to 130 DOC, at roughly 20 to 40 g (count about 25 to 50 per kg), depending on the market. Harvest early if there is a disease outbreak, or a serious water-quality or weather risk; delaying harvest in these cases increases loss.

## Estimating harvest date

Harvest date can be estimated from the current ABW, the recent ADG, and the target size.

Formula: days to target = (target ABW - current ABW) / expected ADG.

Remember that ADG usually falls as shrimp get larger, so use a slightly lower ADG for the final weeks, and treat the result as an estimate.

## Harvest performance numbers

- Final ABW and count per kg
- Total harvest weight (kg) and yield per acre
- Final survival %
- Harvest FCR = total feed used / total harvest weight
- Culture duration (DOC at harvest)

## Improving harvest results

Better survival, controlled feeding, stable water quality, healthy seed, and good aeration are the main drivers of both a lower FCR and a higher harvest weight. Partial harvests can reduce biomass load in the pond when DO or water quality is under pressure.
