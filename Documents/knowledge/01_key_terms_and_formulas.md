# Key Terms and Formulas

General reference for shrimp (Litopenaeus vannamei) pond culture. These are definitions and typical guidelines, not data of any specific pond.

## ABW - Average Body Weight

ABW is the average weight of one shrimp, in grams (g). It is measured by sampling shrimp with a cast net and dividing the total sample weight by the number of shrimp. ABW is the main number used to track growth.

## ADG - Average Daily Growth

ADG is how many grams the average shrimp gains per day.

Formula: ADG (g/day) = (ABW at this sampling - ABW at previous sampling) / number of days between the two samplings.

Weekly growth is roughly ADG x 7. In well-managed vannamei ponds, ADG is often around 0.1 to 0.3 g/day depending on size and stage; it normally slows down in the last weeks before harvest.

## DOC - Days of Culture

DOC is the number of days since the pond was stocked with seed (post-larvae). DOC 1 is the stocking day. Feed, growth and disease risk are always read against DOC.

## FCR - Feed Conversion Ratio

FCR tells how many kilograms of feed were needed to produce 1 kg of shrimp.

Formula: FCR = total feed given (kg) / shrimp biomass gained (kg).

A lower FCR is better. For vannamei, an FCR of about 1.2 to 1.5 is generally considered good, 1.5 to 1.8 is acceptable but should be watched, and above 1.8 is high (feed is being wasted or growth/survival is poor). A high FCR can be caused by overfeeding, poor feed quality, poor water quality, disease, or low survival.

## Biomass

Biomass is the total weight of shrimp in the pond, in kg.

Formula: Biomass (kg) = ABW (g) x estimated number of live shrimp / 1000.

Biomass is an estimate because the number of live shrimp is estimated from survival.

## Survival

Survival % = (estimated live shrimp / shrimp stocked) x 100. In many commercial vannamei ponds, 70 to 85 percent survival is a typical target; lower survival raises FCR and cost per kg.

## Stocking density

Stocking density is the number of seed stocked per unit area, for example PL per square metre. Higher density needs better aeration, water exchange and feed management.

## Count (size grading)

Count is the number of shrimp per kg. Count = 1000 / ABW (g). Example: ABW 25 g gives count 40 per kg.

## PL - Post-larvae

PL is the seed stocked in the pond, usually stage PL10 to PL15 or older. Seed quality (PCR-tested, healthy, uniform) strongly affects survival and growth.
